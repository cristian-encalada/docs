## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Symbolic Act 3 ending
The system SHALL present Act 3 (The Horizon) as a closing scene (cliff/dock facing a European skyline) with no combat or puzzle mechanic gating progress.

#### Scenario: Reaching The Horizon
- **WHEN** the player reaches the end of the level
- **THEN** the closing scene renders with no fight or puzzle mechanic required to proceed

### Requirement: Real contact CTA
The system SHALL provide a "Continue the journey" interactable node at the end of Act 3 that opens a panel with real, working contact links (email, LinkedIn, resume) rather than placeholder text.

#### Scenario: Opening the contact panel
- **WHEN** the player interacts with the closing node
- **THEN** a panel opens containing working email, LinkedIn, and resume links
